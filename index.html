How I work

A process, not a guessing game

Seven steps, from first call to clear reporting. You always know what is happening and why.

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    The process starts when you book a call. It has two parts. First, a short introductory call. Then a proper audit, where we cover your goals for your brand, what growth looks like for you, your target audience, your competitors, and your current numbers.

  2. 02

    Pricing

    After the discovery call, I send you my price list and contract. Review the tiers, choose the one that fits your needs, and make your payment.

  3. 03

    Contract Signing

    Once payment is made, we sign the contract and officially begin working together.

  4. 04

    Strategy

    We turn the audit into a plan. This covers brand positioning, content pillars, formats, and posting cadence.

  5. 05

    Profile Optimization

    I optimize your profiles on the platforms that matter for you, whether that’s Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or others.

  6. 06

    Execution

    We put the strategy into action and start creating and posting content.

  7. 07

    Reports

    You receive regular reports tracking performance against the goals set in the discovery call.
